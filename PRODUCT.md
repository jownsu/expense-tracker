# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person — the owner — tracking their own money. They replace a monthly Google Sheet ritual (duplicate sheet, type income, expenses, savings). Most entries happen on a phone, in the moment, one-handed: right after paying for something. Review and month-close happen on a laptop or a calm phone session at month-end.

## Product Purpose

Percentage-based budget envelopes (Needs / Wants / Savings / Misc), savings accounts with running balances, and a savings prediction — the spreadsheet's model, without the spreadsheet's friction. Success: logging an expense takes seconds, and "how much is left" is answered at a glance. Single currency (₱ PHP).

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. A personal ledger, not a coach. Numbers are the hero; the interface stays quiet around them. No cheerleading, no guilt — plain statements of what is.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: card grids, hero-metric tiles, purple gradients, icon+heading+text cards.
- Spreadsheet clones: gridlines everywhere, cramped cells — the thing being escaped.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first.** Amounts are the most legible thing on any screen; typography for figures (tabular, aligned) is a core concern, not a detail.
2. **Seconds to log.** The add-expense path is short, thumb-reachable, and never blocked by decoration.
3. **Quiet confidence.** Restraint over flourish; color carries meaning (left vs. over), not mood.
4. **Honest state.** Offline, cold-start, and closed-month states are stated plainly, never hidden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: ≥4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus, keyboard operable, ≥44px tap targets on mobile, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, color never the sole signal for over/under budget.
